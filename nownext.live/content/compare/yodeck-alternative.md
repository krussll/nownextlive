## Why Enterprise Digital Signage Fails for Live Events

### 1. Hardware Dependencies & Setup Friction
Yodeck requires ordering, provisioning, and pairing dedicated media players (like Raspberry Pi devices) for every single display screen. At temporary event venues, pop-up sports tournaments, or rented halls, installing hardware dongles, configuring local Wi-Fi pairing codes, and managing media players wastes hours of setup time.

With **NowNext.live**, you simply open the venue display URL in the built-in browser of any Smart TV, iPad, firestick, or laptop.

### 2. Static Playlists vs Dynamic Event Schedules
Digital signage platforms treat screens as media players playing looped video or image playlists. When a badminton match finishes early or a conference keynote runs 20 minutes over, changing a Yodeck playlist requires opening a complex design canvas, editing static text, rendering assets, and pushing media updates to hardware devices.

**NowNext.live** gives event directors a fast mobile controller interface. Tap once to advance Court 2 to the next match, and the display updates live in real time.

### 3. High Costs for Temporary or Seasonal Events
Paying recurring monthly hardware player licenses for screens that are only used during weekend tournaments, monthly club nights, or annual conferences is inefficient. NowNext.live offers transparent pricing designed specifically for event organizers.
