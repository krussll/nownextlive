## The Friction of Using Google Slides for Event Schedules

### 1. Manual Slide Editing & Presentation Interruptions
When a speaker runs overtime or a sports match is delayed, updating a Google Slide presentation requires an organizer to locate the presentation laptop, exit full-screen mode, edit text fields, re-align boxes, and restart the slideshow. During this process, venue screens display messy desktop windows and editing tools.

With **NowNext.live**, event controllers update session times or advance fixtures from their smartphone. The change broadcasts smoothly to all venue screens in the background with zero disruption.

### 2. No Automatic "NOW & NEXT" Progression
Google Slides cannot highlight what is happening *right now* vs *what is coming up next* dynamically based on real-time event flow. Participants looking at a static agenda slide must manually read line items and check their watches to figure out where they should be.

NowNext.live displays clean, prominent **NOW** and **NEXT** status cards tailored for venue visibility from 30 feet away.

### 3. Screen Casting & Cable Clutter
Running Google Slides across multiple venue monitors often requires running HDMI cables, wireless display dongles, or mirror casting laptops. If the main laptop goes to sleep or disconnects, venue screens go black.

NowNext.live runs directly inside web browsers on Smart TVs, iPads, tablets, or Fire TV sticks. Once opened, screens stay live and sync automatically over Wi-Fi or mobile data.
