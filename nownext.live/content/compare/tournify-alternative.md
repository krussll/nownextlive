## Where Tournify Falls Short on Matchday

### 1. Lack of High-Visibility Court-Side Displays
While bracket managers show who plays who in round 3, players standing outside Court 4 need to see two things clearly: **Who is playing right NOW**, and **Who is playing NEXT**. 

NowNext.live provides dedicated high-contrast schedule cards optimized for Smart TVs, iPads, and monitors positioned outside courts and pitches.

### 2. Desk-Bound Matchday Updates
Tournament desk software often forces referees or volunteers to sit behind a laptop at a central desk to update match progress. 

With NowNext.live, tournament directors can walk between courts with a smartphone. Tapping to complete a match or shift a fixture instantly updates court-side monitors, clubhouse screens, and player phones.

### 3. App Download Requirements & Friction for Spectators
Asking players, parents, and spectators to download dedicated apps or navigate complex tournament portals creates friction. NowNext.live allows anyone in the venue to scan a QR code on their phone to open a auto-updating schedule in their web browser.
