## Challenges of Running School Sports Days

Physical education teachers and PTA organizers face unique challenges on sports day:

* **Simultaneous Track & Field Events**: Running 100m sprints on the track while high jump and javelin occur on field areas requires multi-location coordination.
* **Weather & Paper Chaos**: Paper schedules posted on outdoor fences blow away or get wet.
* **Parents & Students Missing Events**: Students miss event calls when they can't see which age group race is currently marshalling.

## Step-by-Step: Organizing Your Sports Day Display for Free

Here is how to set up dynamic sports day screens in under 3 minutes:

### Step 1: Set Up Track & Field Event Columns
On a phone or laptop, navigate to [/r/generate](https://nownext.live/r/generate).

1. Name your event (e.g. *Oakridge School Annual Sports Day*).
2. Create columns for event zones (e.g. *Main Track*, *Field Zone A*, *Field Zone B*, *Presentation Stage*).
3. Input active heats under **NOW** (e.g. *Year 7 100m Heats*) and upcoming races under **NEXT** (e.g. *Year 8 100m Heats*).

---

### Step 2: Set Up Outdoor Screens or Shared Links
Display event timetables across multiple locations on school grounds:

#### Option A: Spectator TV / Battery-Powered Monitors
Connect an outdoor TV, projector, or large tablet screen in the spectator seating area to your room's display URL.

#### Option B: QR Code Posters for Parents & Students
Print posters featuring a QR code linked to your read-only display URL. Parents and students can scan the code to view live track updates on their personal phones anywhere on the field.

---

### Step 3: Update Events Live from the Trackside
Teachers and marshals update race progression on the fly:

1. Open the **Controller URL** on any teacher's smartphone or iPad.
2. Tap **Update** as heats finish to advance to the next event.
3. All spectator screens and parent smartphones update instantly in sub-100 milliseconds.

> **Pro Tip:** Assign different PE staff members mobile access to update their specific zone (e.g. Track Teacher updates Track column; Field Teacher updates Field column).
