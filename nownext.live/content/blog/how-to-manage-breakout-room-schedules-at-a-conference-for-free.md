## Why Paper Signs and Static TV Slides Fail

Traditional conference breakout room signage introduces several friction points:

* **Outdated Printed Door Sheets**: When a speaker swaps time slots or a panel discussion runs 10 minutes over, printed schedules become inaccurate immediately.
* **Tethered Laptops & Cables**: Running HDMI cables from a laptop to a wall monitor outside every room ties up staff computers and creates tripping hazards in busy corridors.
* **Excessive Equipment Rental Costs**: Commercial digital signage providers charge daily rental fees for hardware boxes that simply render static text.

### Step 2: Launch Displays Outside Breakout Rooms
