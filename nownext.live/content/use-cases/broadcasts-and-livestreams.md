## High-Pressure Bottlenecks in Live Studio Production

Live broadcast and streaming environments operate under extreme time pressure, leading to frequent operational headaches:

* **Talent Missing Segment Cues**: Presenters get wrapped up in conversations and miss off-camera hand signals or teleprompter cues.
* **Unscripted Overruns**: Live interviews or product demos run over allocated time, forcing producers to frantically restructure remaining show segments.
* **Complex AV Timing Hardware**: Traditional studio timing systems require expensive hardware, complex software licenses, and dedicated AV engineering support.
