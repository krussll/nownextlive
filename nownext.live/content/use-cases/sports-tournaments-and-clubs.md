# Real-Time Court & Pitch Scheduling for Sports Tournaments

Organizing a multi-court tournament or club night is a high-stakes balancing act. When matches run long, courts get re-assigned, or players miss their call-up times, paper printouts and whiteboards quickly fail. **NowNext.live** replaces manual schedule chaos with instant, synchronized display signage across every court and screen in your venue.

---

## Content Outline

### 1. Introduction: The Frustrations of Matchday Coordination
- **The Problem**: Paper schedules pasted on walls become obsolete within 30 minutes of play starting.
- **The Toll on Organizers**: Volunteers and tournament directors spend half their day answering repetitive questions ("Which court am I on?", "Am I playing next?").
- **The Spectator & Player Experience**: Players crowd around control desks, missing warm-ups or arriving late to assigned courts.

### 2. Key Challenges Solved by NowNext.live
- **Dynamic Delay Handling**: Adjusting a match duration on your phone automatically shifts downstream schedules.
- **Multi-Court Visibility**: Showing dedicated "Now" & "Next" fixtures outside Court 1, Court 2, Court 3, etc., while broadcasting a master overview board in the clubhouse bar or lobby.
- **Mobile QR Access**: Allowing players to scan a single venue QR code to track court assignments live on their personal smartphones.

### 3. Core Features for Tournament Organizers
- **Single Controller Dashboard**: Manage 2 to 20+ courts from one master phone or tablet interface.
- **Zero App Downloads**: No registration or app store installation required for players or screen devices.
- **On-Air & Status Toggles**: Mark courts as "Live", "Warmup", "In Progress", or "Completed" in real time.

### 4. Step-by-Step Setup Guide for Your Next Tournament
1. **Create Your Tournament Board**: Initialize your event on NowNext.live in 30 seconds.
2. **Define Your Courts/Spaces**: Add your court names (e.g., Court A, Court B, Pitch 1, Table 4).
3. **Load Match Schedules**: Input scheduled matches and player names.
4. **Deploy Displays**: Launch read-only display URLs on Smart TVs or tablets mounted outside courts.
