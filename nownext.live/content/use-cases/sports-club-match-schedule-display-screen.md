## Common Match Day Problems at Sports Clubs

Whether running lawn bowls, tennis leagues, padel tournaments, squash matches, or martial arts meets, club committees face the same frustrations:

* **Crowded Noticeboards**: Players and spectators gather around paper schedules, blocking walkways and missing match updates.
* **Match Delay Confusion**: When a game runs long, manually erasing and rewriting times on whiteboards wastes valuable volunteer time.
* **Missed Call-ups**: Players miss their match start times because they couldn't hear PA announcements or check updated court assignments.
