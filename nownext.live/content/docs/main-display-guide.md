# Main Display Output Guide

The **Main Display** is the primary public display view in NowNext.live (`/r/[id]`). It presents a clear multi-column overview of all spaces/courts in your venue, showing what is happening **NOW** and what is coming **NEXT**.

---

## Layout Architecture

- **Header Bar**: Displays your custom event title, active local time, and venue status.
- **Space Cards Grid**:
  - Each space (e.g. *Court 1*, *Court 2*, *Court 3*) gets a high-contrast column card.
  - **NOW Section**: Highlighted green/indigo card showing active match, player names, and start time.
  - **NEXT Section**: Subdued card displaying the upcoming fixture so players know where to queue next.

---

## Setting Up Main Display on Venue Screens

### 1. Mounting & Resolution
- **Screen Size**: 43" to 65" 1080p or 4K Smart TVs recommended.
- **Placement**: Mount in central high-traffic areas such as venue entrances, spectator bleachers, or refreshment areas.

### 2. Launching the Display
- Open the TV's browser (e.g. Chrome, Silk, Samsung Internet).
- Enter your event's Main Display URL (`https://nownext.live/r/[your_event_id]`).
- Enable full-screen mode (`F11` or browser full-screen button).

---

## Key Features & Customization

- **Real-Time Sync**: Updates instantly when the organizer advances a session in the controller. No refreshing needed.
- **Responsive Columns**:
  - 1 to 4 spaces: Displays spacious large-font cards.
  - 5+ spaces: Automatically arranges into an easy-to-read grid.
- **Offline Banner**: If venue Wi-Fi disconnects, a discrete indicator alerts staff while keeping existing schedule cards visible.
