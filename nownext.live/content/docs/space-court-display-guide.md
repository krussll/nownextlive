# Space & Court Display Guide

The **Space Display** (or **Court Display**) is a focused view dedicated to a single space (`/r/[id]?space=[space_id]`). Instead of showing all courts in the venue, it dedicates 100% of screen real estate to the current and upcoming matches for one specific court or room.

---

## Why Use Space Displays?

In multi-court tournaments (badminton, squash, tennis, martial arts) or multi-track conferences, participants often crowd entrance screens to find out when their match is up. 

Placing a tablet or TV outside **Court 4** showing only **Court 4's** schedule solves crowding and lets players check their warm-up time at a glance.

---

## Features & Visual Design

- **Ultra-Large Typography**: Player names, court numbers, and match statuses are visible from 15+ feet away.
- **Current Match ("NOW") Banner**: Prominent card displaying current competitors and match category.
- **On Deck ("NEXT") Card**: Clearly shows the next pair of competitors to arrive at the court entrance.
- **Upcoming List**: Displays 2–3 subsequent matches lined up for the court.

---

## Setup Instructions for Court Tablets

1. **Obtain the Space URL**:
   - In Controller → **Output Links** → Select target space (e.g. *Court 1*).
   - Copy the direct Space link or scan QR code with tablet.
2. **Mount Tablet Court-Side**:
   - Position tablet on a tripod stand, table mount, or wall bracket near court entrance net.
3. **Lock Browser in Kiosk Mode**:
   - Enable **Guided Access** on iPad or **App Pinning** on Android to prevent unauthorized navigation.
