# Getting Started with NowNext.live

NowNext.live is a real-time event, sports tournament, and conference schedule signage platform. It replaces manual whiteboards, paper schedules, and verbal announcements with live-updating digital displays broadcast across any internet-connected device.

---

## What is NowNext.live?

NowNext.live keeps participants, players, spectators, and staff synchronized to the exact second. Whenever an organizer updates a match time, moves a speaker, or changes court assignments, all connected displays update instantly without requiring a page refresh.

- **Zero App Downloads**: Runs directly in standard web browsers (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Fire TV browser).
- **Multi-Screen Synchronization**: Control dozens of court or room screens from a single phone, tablet, or laptop.
- **High-Visibility UI**: High-contrast, easy-to-read "NOW" and "NEXT" schedule cards optimized for viewing across venue halls.

---

## 3-Step Setup Guide

Getting your venue or tournament running with digital signage takes under 3 minutes:

1. **Create an Event**
   - Click **Generate Event** or launch a custom event room in your organizer dashboard.
   - Set your event name, define your spaces (e.g. Court 1, Court 2, Main Stage), and add your initial session schedule.

2. **Open the Controller Dashboard**
   - Use the **Controller** interface on your phone or laptop to control match statuses, advance sessions, or adjust times in real time.

3. **Deploy Display Outputs**
   - Open **Output Links** in the controller toolbar to copy URLs or generate QR codes.
   - Launch the **Main Display** on entrance Smart TVs, or dedicated **Space/Court Displays** on tablets positioned outside specific rooms/courts.

## Core Concepts & Terminology

### 1. Spaces (Courts / Rooms / Stages)
- A **Space** represents a physical location in your venue where activities happen concurrently (e.g. *Court 1*, *Court 2*, *Seminar Room A*, *Main Stage*).
- Each space displays what is happening **NOW** (current active session) and what is coming **NEXT** (upcoming scheduled session).

### 2. Sessions & Matches
- **Sessions** are individual schedule items (e.g. *Match #101: Smith vs. Jones*, *Keynote Address: AI in Sports*).
- Sessions hold details including title, category/division, scheduled start time, duration, and status.

### 3. Controller vs. Display Outputs
- **Controller**: The private organizer dashboard used to manage spaces, advance matches, edit titles, and monitor connected device counts.
- **Display Outputs**: Read-only, real-time updated views opened on venue monitors, Smart TVs, tablets, or spectator smartphones.
