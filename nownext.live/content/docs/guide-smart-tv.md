# Setting Up Displays on Smart TVs & Fire Sticks

Turn any commercial TV or HDMI monitor into a live-updating schedule signage board using built-in web browsers or low-cost media sticks.

---

## Method 1: Amazon Fire TV Stick (Recommended)

1. Connect Fire TV Stick to TV HDMI port and connect to venue Wi-Fi.
2. Open **Amazon Silk Browser** from the Fire TV home screen.
3. Enter your Main Display or Space URL (`https://nownext.live/r/[event_id]`).
4. Click Menu button on Fire TV remote → Select **Full Screen**.
5. **Prevent Sleep Mode**: Go to Fire TV Settings → Display & Sounds → Saver → Set Start Time to **Never** (or use a free kiosk app like *Fully Kiosk Browser*).

---

## Method 2: Samsung Tizen & LG webOS Smart TVs

1. Open the built-in **Web Browser** application on your Smart TV home menu.
2. Navigate to your NowNext display URL.
3. Tap the full-screen expansion icon in the top right corner of the browser navbar.
4. Set TV Screen Sleep timer to OFF in TV System Settings to prevent energy saver turn-off during events.

---

## Pro Tips for Venue Digital Signage

- **Bookmark the URL**: Save the display URL to TV browser bookmarks so staff can launch signage with one click.
- **Power On Auto-Launch**: Commercial TVs can be set to automatically open the last used browser tab upon powering on.
- **Use High-Contrast Mode**: If TV displays under direct sunlight or bright hall lights, use Light Mode for maximum visibility.
