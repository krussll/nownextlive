# Using Tablets & iPads as Court Signage

Tablets (such as Apple iPads or Samsung Galaxy Tabs) are ideal court-side displays for sports tournaments due to their compact size, battery power, and crisp high-resolution touchscreens.

---

## Setting Up Apple iPads (iOS)

### 1. Enable Guided Access (Kiosk Mode)
1. Go to **Settings** → **Accessibility** → **Guided Access**.
2. Toggle **Guided Access** to ON and set a passcode.
3. Open Safari and navigate to your target Space Display URL (`/r/[id]?space=[space_id]`).
4. Triple-click the iPad Power / Home button to start **Guided Access**.
5. Disables touch interaction and prevents players or passersby from closing the browser display.

---

## Setting Up Android Tablets

### 1. Enable App Pinning
1. Go to **Settings** → **Security** → **App Pinning** (or *Pin Windows*).
2. Open Chrome with your Space Display URL open.
3. Swipe up to Recents view → Tap Chrome app icon → Select **Pin this app**.

---

## Mounting & Power Management

- **Continuous Power**: Connect tablets to power banks or wall chargers during long 8-hour tournament days.
- **Auto-Lock Timeout**: Set iPad Screen Lock to **Never** (Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → *Never*).
- **Mounting Stands**: Use heavy-duty tablet clamps or tripod mounts attached to court posts or scorer tables.
